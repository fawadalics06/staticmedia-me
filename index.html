Fawad Ali, trading as Static Media is a marketing and advertising services company. We plan, build and manage digital advertising campaigns for HVAC companies across the United States. This page explains what we do, how we communicate with the businesses we work with, and how our messaging program works.

What We Do

Our services

We are a marketing services provider. We do not install, sell, finance or service HVAC equipment, and we are not a licensed contractor.

01

Discovery

We learn about the business we're working with, the services they offer, the areas they cover, and the capacity they have.

02

Campaign Build

We design advertising creative and build the targeting, tracking and landing experience for the campaign.

03

Campaign Management

We run and adjust campaigns on an ongoing basis, monitoring performance and making changes as the data comes in.

04

Reporting

We report back on how campaigns are performing so the business can make its own decisions about what to do next.
